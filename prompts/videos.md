# Video prompts (Veo)

Short clips for scroll-scrubbed heroes. The page plays the clip forward as the visitor scrolls, so:

- **5–8 seconds** is enough.
- **Slow, continuous motion** with no cuts. Cuts look jumpy when scrubbed.
- **The camera moves one way only** (push in, or slide sideways).
- **No text, no faces.**

These clips are reusable across many boutiques of the same template and colour family, so generate once and reuse.

---

## Silk unfurling

```
Slow-motion shot of a length of {primary colour name} silk saree with {accent colour name}
zari border unfurling across a dark wooden table, fabric rippling gently.
Camera slowly pushes in. Warm, low-key lighting, luxurious, cinematic. No people, no text.
8 seconds, continuous single shot.
```

## Needle and thread

```
Extreme close-up, slow motion: a needle pulling golden thread through {primary colour name}
fabric in a wooden embroidery frame, creating maggam work. Camera slowly tracks sideways.
Warm natural light, shallow depth of field. No faces, no text. 6 seconds, single shot.
```

## Fabric fall

```
Slow-motion shot of several silk dupattas in {primary colour name}, {accent colour name}
and cream falling and settling softly against a dark background. Camera static.
Elegant, cinematic lighting. No people, no text. 6 seconds, single shot.
```

## Tips

- Download the highest quality available, and save as `boutiques/<slug>/photos/ai-hero.mp4`.
- Also save one clean frame as `ai-hero-poster.jpg`. It shows while the video loads and on phones with reduced motion.
